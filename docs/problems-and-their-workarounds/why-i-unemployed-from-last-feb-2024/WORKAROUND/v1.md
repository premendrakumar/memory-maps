# Workarounds: Why I am not able to get a job opportunity?

## **Sleep timely and `Stay calm`**
- Meditate
- Do morning exercises
- Talk with family members, just casually.

---
## `Review` Existing situation and data
- Make a practice to review `What-is-in-the-plate` and make `effective-revision` plan
- Have some information about new interview-schedules, companies and JD. Prioritize your preferences and expectations from next employing/interview companies.

---

## **`Prioritize` tasks coming in mind**
- Do not jump to a new task randomly. First note down new task in some place (like notebook or somewhere else). Collect as much info about execution of task. Make decision to stick to one-task-at-a-time and act accordingly.

---

## **`Revise` regularly**
- Prepare a weekly `revision-plan` and execute it honestly.
- Notedown problems faced or some new updations in the plan and improvise the `revision-plan` accordingly

---

## **`Practice` coding questions regularly**
- Identify and Collect coding questions
- Use `Tag-mgmt` module data to categorize questions, topics, memory-maps, Chat-GPT conversations into meaningful groups. (No need to create a separate application to categorize existing data).

---

## **`Retrospect`, `Learn` and `Repeat-the-cycle` to improvise the plan**
- Learn from past mistakes and make yourself better by refraining from repeting them in future.
- Forgive yourself for past mistakes.
- Learn from others and have patience to listem their criticises and suggestions carefully.
